Privacy policy

In short: CarDep uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking, and we do not collect personal information about you.

What is stored on your device

If your browser supports it, the site keeps copies of pages, pictures and fonts in the browser cache, so that CarDep loads faster and works offline. None of it is sent to us, and you remove it by clearing the site data in your browser.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc. in the USA. Like all web servers, GitHub records technical data such as your IP address when you visit, for security and operations. This is covered by GitHub's privacy statement.

Fonts and links

The fonts are on the same server as the site, so nothing is sent to Google or other third parties when you view CarDep. Links to other sites, such as sources and photographers' pages, only open when you click them.

The AI chat

When the chat is switched on, the following applies. Your question and what you see in the calculator at that moment (your settings and the cars in the list) are sent to CarDep's server and on to Anthropic, which provides the AI model Claude, so that an answer can be written. Anthropic processes the data under its privacy policy. We do not store the conversations. Do not write personal information in the chat. To prevent abuse, the server counts how many questions come from each IP address within an hour. This is only kept in memory and is not stored.

Your rights

As we do not store personal information about you, there is normally nothing to hand over or delete. If you have questions, contact us. You can also contact the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection, IMY.
